# Majora City: Story Bible

## Premise

After the events of *Ocarina of Time*, Link rides Epona out of Hyrule searching for a lost friend. In vanilla
Majora's Mask he is ambushed in the Lost Woods. In **Majora City** he leaves the woods on an old trade road,
and the road turns into asphalt.

Termina has been *rewritten*. When the Skull Kid put on Majora's Mask, the mask did more than pull the moon
toward the land. It warped Termina into the shape of its own appetite: a roaring, neon, money-hungry
metropolis called **Majora City**. Clock Town grew into a skyline. The townsfolk still have their names and
faces and the lives they always had, but now they are hustlers, cops, club owners, union bosses and
smugglers, and they half-remember a quieter town.

The moon still hangs over the skyline, grinning. In 72 hours it will fall.

Under the city, Majora's influence flows through **Majora Corp**, a company nobody can remember founding.
It holds the deeds to the Clock Tower skyscraper, the Festival of Time, the four boroughs and, apparently,
the moon. Four "partners" run the boroughs for it: **Odolwa**, **Goht**, **Gyorg** and **Twinmold**,
reimagined from the vanilla dungeon bosses as crime lords and the monstrous machines they control.

Link arrives with nothing but a horse, a sword and an ocarina, and the city takes all three within an hour.

## Tone

- **GTA III / Vice City** pulp: big personalities, double-crosses, funny radio ads, neon nights.
- **Majora's Mask** melancholy: every gangster is also a person with a schedule, a fear and a mask they hide behind.
  Missions should regularly end on a quiet, human beat.
- Link stays a silent protagonist. **Tatl** does the talking: sarcastic, street-smart, and secretly scared.

## Prologue: "Welcome to Majora City"

| # | Mission | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Highway to the City** *(opening cutscene)* | Link gallops on Epona out of the woods into Termina at dawn. The fields are crossed by paved roads; ahead, behind the old Clock Town walls, skyscrapers climb into the clouds. Title card: **MAJORA CITY**. The full storyboard is in [`opening-cutscene.md`](opening-cutscene.md). |
| P2 | **Impounded** | At the South Gate (now a toll checkpoint), two MCPD officers stop Link. *"No livestock inside city limits, pal."* Epona is hauled off to the **Romani Impound Lot**. A furious fairy, Tatl, watches from a lamppost and laughs. |
| P3 | **Mugged** | In the alley behind the Clock Tower, the Skull Kid's crew jumps Link. The Skull Kid steals the Ocarina of Time and uses the mask to curse Link into a **Deku Scrub**. In the chaos, Tatl is left behind and gets stuck with the new kid. |
| P4 | **The Salesman** | Inside the Clock Tower's service level, the **Happy Mask Salesman** (just "the Salesman" on the streets) makes an offer: *"Get back what was stolen from me, and I'll help you get back what was stolen from you. You have 72 hours."* He becomes Link's first contact and quest-giver. |

## Act I: "Small Time" (Downtown Majora City)

Link learns the city as a Deku Scrub, earns a reputation, and gets his body back.

| # | Mission | Contact | Summary / new mechanic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Rooftop Delivery** | Business Scrub | Deliver parcels across rooftops by Deku Flower launch. Teaches traversal and the **minimap radar**. |
| 2 | **Bombers' Code** | Jim (Bombers) | Win a chase-tag race against the Bombers kids' gang. Reward: the **Bombers' Notebook**, now the **Job Book** (mission log, contacts, schedules). |
| 3 | **Fountain of Youth** | Great Fairy | Return the stray fairy in Northside Park. Reward: magic. |
| 4 | **Midnight on the Roof** | the Salesman | On the final night, climb the Clock Tower skyscraper and confront the Skull Kid. Get the Ocarina back, learn the **Song of Time**, and go back to day one. |
| 5 | **Clean Slate** | the Salesman | Learn the **Song of Healing** and get human form back. Tutorial for what persists across cycles (bank, masks, businesses, Respect). |
| 6 | **Night Watch** | Captain Viscen (MCPD) | Ride along with the city watch on a night patrol. Introduces **Heat** from the cop's side and unlocks the *Night Watch* side job. |
| 7 | **Milk Bar Tab** | Madame Aroma | The Mayor's wife hires Link to find her missing son, Kafei. Opens the **Kafei Case** noir storyline (see Act II). |
| 8 | **Grand Theft Epona** | Romani | Break Epona out of the Romani Impound Lot at night (stealth). Learn **Epona's Song**, which now works as a whistle to call her anywhere outdoors. |

| 10 | **City Hall** | Mayor Dotour | The guard wants to evacuate; Majora Corp wants the Festival of Time to go ahead. Link overhears the name of Majora Corp's four "partners". Opens the four boroughs. |

## Act II: "Turf" (The Four Boroughs)

The boroughs can be played in any order, as in vanilla. Each has a **local ally faction**, a **crime boss**,
a **temple reimagined as a hideout**, a **transformation mask**, and a borough-exclusive radio station.

### A. Woodfall Bayou *(Southern Swamp + Woodfall)*
An Everglades-style swamp with boat tours, stilt shacks and a poisoned river.

| Mission | Summary |
|---|---|
| **Bayou Tours** | Koume and Kotake run airboat tours and a potion business. Their tour boat is hijacked, so Link rides shotgun and gets it back (boat archery). |
| **Monkey Business** | Rescue the Deku Palace's monkey informant before he's "made an example of". |
| **The Deku Trading Company** | The Deku King runs the swamp's legitimate business and suspects Odolwa of poisoning the water to drive down land prices. |
| **The Jungle Club** *(Woodfall Temple)* | Odolwa's hideout is a ruined temple turned into an underground club. |
| **Boss: Odolwa, the Bayou Kingpin** | A masked warlord fought in an arena of drums and fire. |

### B. Snowhead Heights *(Mountain Village + Snowhead)*
A frozen steel town. Goron union workers, foundries, and icy mountain highways.

| Mission | Summary |
|---|---|
| **Ghost Rider** | Darmani, the late leader of the "Rolling Thunder" Goron biker gang, asks Link to finish what he couldn't. Reward: **Goron Mask** (Goron roll is the hack's motorcycle). |
| **Strike!** | The Goron Steelworkers' Union walks out on Goht's foundry. Link escorts the picket line through the blizzard. |
| **Snowhead Grand Prix** | Street racing league on the mountain roads. |
| **The Foundry** *(Snowhead Temple)* | Goht's factory: conveyor belts, furnaces and freezing vents. |
| **Boss: Goht, the War Machine** | A mechanical monster-truck chase down the mountain. |

### C. Great Bay Beach *(Great Bay Coast, Zora Cape, Pirates' Fortress)*
The "Vice Beach" of the hack: neon hotels, rock music, beach parties and a smugglers' harbour.

| Mission | Summary |
|---|---|
| **Mikau's Last Song** | The guitarist of the Indigo-Go's is dying on the beach. Reward: **Zora Mask** (fast swimming works as a jet-ski). |
| **Pirate Radio** | Steal the Gerudo Syndicate's transmitter and unlock the **Pirate FM** station. |
| **The Egg Run** | Heist: recover the stolen Zora eggs from the Syndicate fortress. |
| **The Waterworks** *(Great Bay Temple)* | A flooded pumping station under the bay. |
| **Boss: Gyorg, the Thing in the Bay** | The sea monster the Syndicate has been feeding. Fought on a collapsing pier. |

### D. Old Ikana *(Ikana Canyon, Ikana Graveyard, Stone Tower)*
A desert ghost town full of haunted casinos, old money and the hack's most striking landmark: **Stone Tower**,
an inverted skyscraper.

| Mission | Summary |
|---|---|
| **Ghost Town** | Pamela's father is half-Gibdo. Heal him and earn the Gibdo Mask. |
| **The House Always Wins** | Casino heist at Sharp's haunted casino. |
| **Captain's Orders** | Captain Keeta's skeleton army runs protection in the graveyard. Earn the Captain's Hat. |
| **Upside Down** *(Stone Tower Temple)* | Climb the tower, then flip the city's financial district upside down. |
| **Boss: Twinmold, the Night Trains** | Two possessed subway trains in the tunnels under Stone Tower. |

### The Kafei Case *(runs across Acts I–II)*
A noir subplot told through the vanilla Anju & Kafei schedule. Kafei is a private eye who wears a mask to
investigate Majora Corp. He was robbed of the **Sun's Mask** by **Sakon**, the city's biggest fence.
Link and Kafei raid **Sakon's Hideout** together (a split-screen-style co-op heist, as in vanilla).
The case ends with the Anju & Kafei wedding at the Stock Pot Hotel on the final night (reward: Couple's Mask)
and Kafei's dossier: *the Clock Tower's deed is signed with a mask, not a name.*

## Act III: "The Tower" (Final Night)

| # | Mission | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Oath to Order** | With all four boroughs freed, their guardians (the Giants) answer the call. |
| 2 | **Majora Corp** | Infiltrate the Clock Tower skyscraper, Majora Corp's HQ, floor by floor: office floors, the server room (the clock's gears) and the executive suite. |
| 3 | **The Penthouse in the Sky** | The moon's interior as a dreamscape: four masked children and four short "greatest hits" missions recapping each borough. |
| 4 | **Majora** | Final boss in three phases: Majora's Mask, then Majora's Incarnation (now dancing under disco lights), then Majora's Wrath. |

### Ending
Dawn breaks over the skyline. As the moon disappears, the skyscrapers turn translucent and the city folds back
into Clock Town, though not completely. The Clock Tower keeps a single neon sign. Link rides Epona out along
the old road while the Salesman watches from the walls: *"Whatever... you've met with a terrible fate,
haven't you? But it seems you've also made a few friends."*

### Post-game
Free roam with all side activities. Reaching 100% city completion unlocks the **Fierce Deity's Mask** for
free roam and the **Kingpin** ending tag on the file select.

## Persistence across the 72-hour cycle

| Persists (Song of Time) | Resets |
|---|---|
| Story progress, masks, songs, bank balance | Heat level, active missions, carried rupees above the bank |
| Owned businesses (income accrues per cycle) | Borough boss "turf" (the boss returns, but the Giant's oath is kept) |
| Faction Respect, stunt jumps, tags, Lost Fairies | Side-job streaks (Night Watch level, etc.) |

This mirrors vanilla's rules and gives GTA-style meta-progression (money, property, reputation) a reason to
exist inside a time loop.
