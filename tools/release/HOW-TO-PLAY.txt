MAJORA CITY {LABEL}
===================

A fan-made ROM hack of The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask (N64). This zip contains a PATCH, not the game.
You need your own copy of the game.

WHAT YOU NEED
  A dump of The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask (USA) for the Nintendo 64.
  .z64, .n64 and .v64 files all work. It must be the original, unmodified USA cartridge dump:
  MD5 {MD5}

HOW TO PATCH (easiest)
  1. Open https://www.marcrobledo.com/RomPatcher.js/ in your browser.
  2. "ROM file": pick your Majora's Mask (USA) file.
  3. "Patch file": pick {BPS}
  4. Click "Apply patch" and save the new ROM.

  Other ways: Flips (Windows/macOS/Linux), or from the Majora City folder:
     ./majora-city patch "your-rom.z64" {BPS}

  If the patcher says the checksum doesn't match, your ROM isn't the clean USA version.

HOW TO PLAY
  Open the patched ROM in an N64 emulator (Ares is recommended; RetroArch with the
  Mupen64Plus-Next core also works) or on real hardware with a flash cart and Expansion Pak.
  Start a NEW save file to see the new opening.
