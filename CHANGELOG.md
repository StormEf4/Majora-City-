# Changelog

Every build of the patch is named after `VERSION` (for example `MajoraCity-v0.1.0.bps`) and carries this file in
its share zip. Newest first.

## v0.1.0: "Highway to the City" (milestone M1)

- New opening: a fresh save starts with Link riding Epona across Termina Field at dawn toward the Majora City
  skyline, with narration and a MAJORA CITY title card. Press START to skip. The vanilla prologue follows.
- Procedural skyscrapers rise over the Clock Town walls in Termina Field and around South Clock Town, with
  lit windows at night.
- One-command build: `./majora-city build` produces the patch, a share-ready zip and your playable ROM.
